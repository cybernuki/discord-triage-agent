# Billing

## Refund policy

Refunds are available within 14 days of the first payment on any plan. Approved refunds are processed within five business days and returned to the original payment method.

## Downloading invoices

Invoices are available under Settings, then Billing, then "Invoice history". Each invoice can be downloaded as a PDF.

## Changing your plan

You can upgrade at any time and the new price is prorated. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period.
