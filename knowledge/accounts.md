# Accounts

## Resetting your password

Open the login page and choose "Forgot password". We email a reset link that is valid for 30 minutes. If the email does not arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder and confirm you used the address you registered with.

## Changing your email address

Go to Settings, then Profile, and choose "Change email". You will need to confirm the new address by clicking the link we send to it. Your old address keeps working until the change is confirmed.

## Deleting your account

Settings, then Privacy, then "Delete account". Deletion is permanent and removes your data within 14 days.
